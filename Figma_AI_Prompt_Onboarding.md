# Lumienzo Onboarding Flow - Figma Design Prompt

## Brand Identity
**Colors**: Yellow `#FEFD7F` (CTAs), Black `#18181B` (text/active), Zinc grays (backgrounds)
**Typography**: Modern sans-serif, bold headings, responsive sizing
**Design**: Rounded-xl/rounded-2xl, shadows (sm to 2xl), smooth animations

## Layout
**Desktop**: Split-screen (40% left benefits, 60% right content)
**Mobile**: Stacked with sticky yellow benefit banner

Cards: rounded-2xl/rounded-3xl, selected=black bg/white text/yellow icon bg, unselected=white bg/zinc-200 border, hover scale 1.02. Yellow CTA "Get Started" appears after selection.
